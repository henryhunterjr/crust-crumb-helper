🥖 Krusty Skool Helper v2.1.0

Two buttons sit bottom-right on any Skool or Skooly page.

📋 Paste for review

  1. Copy a DM in the Companion app.
  2. Open the exact conversation with that member.
  3. Click Paste for review, read it, then hit send yourself.

🧭 Capture thread

  1. Open an introductions or discussion thread.
  2. Scroll so the comments you want are loaded.
  3. Click Capture thread to copy it as JSON.
  4. Paste it into Import Introductions in Member Compass.
This extension never sends, posts, replies, or likes anything. It pastes text for your review and reads threads you already have open. Every send stays your call.
